## 4b. Notes on tool-level deviations (not part of the parsed plan fields; see REPORT.md)
- scripts/plan.py's `_fields()` parser matches label lines (e.g. "Palette:", "Fonts:", "Colour
  strategy:") anywhere in the whole document, not scoped to their section, and silently keeps only
  the FIRST occurrence, with no warning about the ones it discards. Section 2's per-direction bullets
  ("- Colour strategy: …", "- Palette: …") used the same label words as section 4's canonical,
  machine-scored fields — an easy thing to do, since deck-plan.md's own section-2 template already
  uses those exact words for the chosen direction. The first draft of this plan had the real Palette
  (section 4) silently discarded in favour of Direction A's descriptive bullet (which used a
  different colour-role wording and produced a real "accent: 0, signal: 3" fail). Renamed the
  section-2 bullets to German synonyms (Farbstrategie/Muster-Varianten/Schrift/Farben) to remove the
  collision. This is a real trap in the template, not a one-off authoring mistake: nothing in
  deck-plan.md or rules-core.md warns that label vocabulary must stay unique across the whole file.
